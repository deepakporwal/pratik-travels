Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour? ▼

You can browse our tour packages on the homepage, select your preferred tour, and click "Book Now". Fill out the booking form with your details, and our team will contact you to confirm the booking and payment.

What payment methods do you accept? ▼

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and bank transfers. A deposit is required to confirm your booking, and the remaining balance is due 30 days before your travel date.

Is travel insurance included? ▼

Yes, basic travel insurance is included in all our packages. This covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost baggage. You can opt for additional coverage if needed.

Can I customize my tour package? ▼

Absolutely! We specialize in customized tours. Contact our team with your preferences, duration, and budget, and we'll create a personalized itinerary just for you.

What is your cancellation policy? ▼

Cancellations made 60+ days before travel receive a full refund. 30-59 days: 50% refund. Less than 30 days: No refund. Travel insurance can cover cancellation if applicable.

Do you provide visa assistance? ▼

For international tours, we provide guidance and can assist with visa documentation. However, the visa process is handled by government agencies, and processing times vary.

Are there group discounts available? ▼

Yes! We offer special discounts for groups of 10 or more travelers. Contact us for a customized group quote at discounted rates.

What should I pack for the tours? ▼

We provide a detailed packing list for each tour based on the destination and season. Generally, bring comfortable clothes, sturdy shoes, sunscreen, and any personal medications.
