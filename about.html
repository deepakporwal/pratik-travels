About Pratik Travels

Learn more about our journey and mission

Who We Are

Pratik Travels is a leading tour and travel company based in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh. Established in 2005, we have over 21 years of experience in the travel industry and have successfully organized countless memorable journeys across the cultural heartland of India.

We specialize in showcasing the rich heritage, natural beauty, and spiritual significance of Madhya Pradesh. From the intricate temples of Khajuraho to the ancient Buddhist monuments of Sanchi, from the majestic Gwalior Fort to the wildlife treasures of Kanha National Park, we offer immersive experiences that connect travelers with India's magnificent past and pristine nature.

Our passion is to help people explore the wonders of Madhya Pradesh and create unforgettable memories. We believe that travel is not just about reaching a destination; it's about the experiences, connections, and personal growth that happen along the way.

Business Info: Established: 2005 • Services: Tourist Taxi, Intracity Taxi, AC Taxi Services.

Our Mission

To provide world-class travel experiences that are affordable, safe, and memorable. We aim to be the most trusted travel partner for every adventurer, culture enthusiast, and leisure traveler.

Our Vision

To inspire wanderlust and foster global understanding by creating meaningful travel experiences. We envision a world where travel connects people, cultures, and hearts.
